Logos from the lobehub icon set (@lobehub/icons-static-png 2.12.0, MIT licence for the set;
each mark is a trademark of its company and is used here to identify the company in news posts).
`mark.png` is the white mono mark, `color.png` the colour mark, `wordmark.png` the white wordmark,
all cropped to their visible pixels. `logos.json` maps company aliases and model-name families to a slug.
Thinking Machines Lab has no icon in that set; its stacked wordmark was cut from its own Inkling 2 Mini
announcement image.
